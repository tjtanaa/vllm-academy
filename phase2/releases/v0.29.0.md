## What learners should notice

Read token accounting before drawing two rigid global scheduler phases. Separate allocation policy from the attention kernel that consumes block mappings. Separate a block's cached identity from its current ownership and reuse eligibility. At the connector boundary, trace when transferred data becomes safe to read and when a buffer becomes safe to recycle.

These are source-reading prompts, not claims that these mechanisms were newly introduced in v0.29.0. The predecessor comparison, full selected runner trace, performance changes and hardware validation remain to be authored.
